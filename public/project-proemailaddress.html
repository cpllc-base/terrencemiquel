SaaS — Live

ProEmailAddress

Every professional email I've seen from a serious business eventually hits the same tell: the domain. A founder can have real revenue, real clients, a real team — and still send the proposal from @gmail.com. Nothing about the business is small. Only the address is.

That's the test at the center of this: would the same words land differently coming from you@yourdomain.com instead of you@gmail.com? They do. Not because anything else changed — because the address is the first signal a stranger reads before they read anything else.

What actually kept people stuck wasn't disagreement — it was the workaround being worse than the problem. Migrating off Gmail, learning a new interface, hiring someone to touch DNS. ProEmailAddress removes that cost: keep the Gmail inbox you already know, and I walk you through the DNS, the mailbox, the aliases — step by step — so mail arrives and sends as your own domain.

It's the main build because it's the one I'm doing in public, from zero: real customers, real setup flows, real mistakes fixed live.
