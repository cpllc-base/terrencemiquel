About Me

Reality teaches. I pay attention.

Hi, I'm Terrence. I'm an introvert — I'd rather go deep on one idea than work a room.

What I go deep on is principles: the ones underneath faith, relationships, health, money, and business. When someone hands me a rule, a doctrine, or a piece of advice, my instinct isn't to accept it or reject it. It's to test it. Run it against reality and see if it holds.

What survives, I build on. What doesn't, I let go.
